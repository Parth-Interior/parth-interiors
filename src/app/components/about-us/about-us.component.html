Our Growth Story

Welcome to Parth Interior, where experience, dedication, and quality craftsmanship come together to deliver exceptional results in every project we undertake.

Founded by Kirit Mistry, our company has been delivering excellence since 2000, built on decades of expertise in the construction and service industry. With a strong foundation of hands-on experience, Kirit Mistry started from humble beginnings and gradually expanded Parth Interior into a full-service turnkey business. Today, we continue to provide innovative solutions and exceptional craftsmanship, making us a trusted name in the industry.

Throughout the years, Kirit Mistry has played a pivotal role in every detail of the business—from managing initial site preparations to overseeing complex projects and providing creative solutions for our clients. With an unwavering commitment to quality and a deep understanding of the entire process, Kirit Mistry has guided the company through numerous successful ventures, building lasting relationships along the way.

At Parth Interior, we are passionate about turning visions into reality. Whether it's a residential project, commercial development, or specialized service, our team brings unmatched expertise, precision, and attention to detail. Our journey from "nothing" to a trusted turnkey provider reflects our dedication to excellence, and we are proud to continue delivering results that exceed expectations.

Thank you for visiting Parth Interior. Let us be a part of your next project, and experience the difference that two decades of experience can make.
